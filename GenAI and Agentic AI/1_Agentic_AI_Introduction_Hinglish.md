# Agentic AI — Aaj Ki Zaroorat

**Agentic AI aaj ke time ki important requirement hai.**

Almost sabhi companies Agentic AI ki taraf move kar rahi hain.

Lekin Agentic AI mein jaane se pehle ek interesting example dekhte hain.

Ya maan lo aapke paas abhi **₹1 crore** hain aur aap 5 saal mein usse **₹20 crore** banana chahte ho.

> Apne paise ko 4–5 saal mein 20x ya 30x kaise kiya ja sakta hai?

# Example 1: Stock Research Using Agentic AI

Maine requirement di:

Mujhe **early-stage micro-cap multibagger stocks** identify karne hain.

**Multibagger stocks** wo stocks hote hain jo relatively short period mein 10x, 20x, 30x ya 40x tak grow kar chuke hain ya kar sakte hain.

Mera target tha India mein aise stocks identify karna jisme approximately **5x–20x return potential** ho.

Main bahut greedy nahi banna chahta tha, isliye maine target 20x par stop kiya.

Assume karo ki main ek **high-risk appetite investor** hoon.

Lekin main random investment nahi karna chahta.

Mujhe ek **structured aur data-driven approach** chahiye.

Isliye maine kaha:

- Screening criteria do
- Fundamental analysis karo
- Quality check karo
- Step-by-step strategy do
- Aur jo bhi relevant analysis hai, woh karo

Maine apni knowledge ke according requirements likh di.

## Agent Mode

Uske baad main ChatGPT ke **Agent Mode** mein gaya.

Maine apna detailed prompt paste kiya aur Agent Mode select kiya.

Historically agar aapko isi type ka research karwana hota, to shayad **20–30 market analysts** ki team ki zaroorat padti.

Agar aap 20–30 experienced market analysts hire karo, jo economics aur investing mein achhe hain, to woh extensive research kar sakte hain.

Lekin ab ChatGPT mein Agent Mode available hai.

Agent Mode ek aisa feature hai jahan system aapke given goal ke according independently kaam karna start karta hai.

Usne kaha ki woh **data-driven approach** follow karega aur relevant data collect karega.

Uske baad computer open hua aur background mein kaam start ho gaya.

Main kuch click nahi kar raha tha.

System khud:

- Websites search kar raha tha
- Links open kar raha tha
- Research papers padh raha tha
- Information collect kar raha tha
- Ek website par information na milne par doosri website par ja raha tha

Yaani jis tarah ek good stock-market analyst research karta hai, Agent Mode waise hi research kar raha tha.

Agar koi page nahi mila, to usne usko ignore karke doosri jagah search karna continue kiya.

# Understanding AI Agents and Agentic AI Systems

Ab tak humne **do AI agents** dekhe:

1. Ek AI agent stock research kar raha tha.
2. Doosra AI agent application ka code likh raha tha.

Ab question hai:

**AI Agent kya hai?**

AI agents ko dekho to ye **autonomous programs** hote hain.

Humne unhe har individual step ka instruction nahi diya.

Humne sirf ek overall prompt/goal diya.

Uske baad ye systems independently kaam karte hain.

AI agent:

- Data perceive kar sakta hai
- Requirement understand kar sakta hai
- Reason kar sakta hai
- Decisions le sakta hai
- Given goal achieve karne ki direction mein action kar sakta hai

Example:

Agar ek website par required information nahi mili, agent ne decide kiya ki doosri website par search karna hai.

Yahi reasoning aur decision-making ka example hai.

Ek goal tha:

**Multibagger stocks identify karna.**

Doosra goal tha:

**End-to-end application create karna.**

Ye AI agent ke examples hain.

Course mein AI agents ko deeply samjha jayega aur multiple AI agents create kiye jayenge.

# AI Agent vs Agentic AI System

AI Agent ek single/simple unit ho sakta hai.

Lekin practical systems mein sirf ek agent nahi hota.

Multiple AI agents ek saath kaam kar sakte hain.

For example:

- Ek agent code likhega
- Doosra agent code test karega
- Teesra agent multiple agents ko orchestrate karega

Is tarah ka system **Agentic AI System** kehlata hai.

Technically Agentic AI ko **Agentic AI System** ke roop mein dekhna useful hai.

Agentic AI system intelligent AI agents ka ek network hota hai jo:

- Collaborate karta hai
- Planning karta hai
- Tasks execute karta hai
- Complex tasks ko dynamically solve karta hai

Agar aap chaar developers ki team ko game banane ke liye task dete ho, to woh ek process follow karenge.

AI agents bhi similar type ka process follow kar sakte hain.

Agar 20 market analysts ki team ko stocks identify karne ke liye kaha jaaye, to AI agents bhi similar workflow follow kar sakte hain.

# Agentic AI Ka Evolution

Agentic AI suddenly ek completely independent field ke roop mein appear nahi hua.

Ye pehle se develop ho rahe multiple areas ka advanced version hai.

Journey ko roughly is tarah dekha gaya:

**Data Gathering → Basic Reporting → Predictive Modeling → Machine Learning → Deep Learning → Generative AI → Agentic AI**

---

# Basic Reporting

Data collect karne ke baad organisations ne basic reports banana start kiya.

Inhe **MIS reports** ya sales reports ke form mein dekha ja sakta hai.

Aaj bhi bahut saare businesses basic reporting par depend karte hain.

Har jagah AI ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

Kabhi-kabhi ek simple report hi decision lene ke liye sufficient hoti hai.

CEOs aur business leaders reports dekhkar decisions lete hain.

Past 20–25 years se basic reporting widely use ho rahi hai.

AI ke aane ke baad bhi reporting completely disappear nahi hogi.

Reporting automate ho sakti hai, lekin reporting ka concept rahega.

Visualization bhi naye forms mein transform ho sakti hai.

# AI Aur Manual Work

AI ka first major use fancy problems solve karna nahi, balki **obvious aur repetitive manual work ko automate karna** hai.

Example ke liye law firm mein AI naye laws create nahi kar raha.

AI basic drafts create kar sakta hai.

Pehle ek experienced lawyer ke saath multiple assistants basic draft prepare karte the.

Ab AI basic draft prepare kar sakta hai.

Isliye manual work ka ek portion directly automate ho raha hai.

Agar aap kisi existing tool, jaise Excel ya PowerPoint, par kaam kar rahe ho, to aapko dekhna chahiye ki us workflow mein AI kaise use ho sakta hai.

Aapko apne existing skills ko AI ke saath upgrade karna hoga.

# Limitations and Challenges of Agentic AI

Agentic AI powerful hai, lekin kuch known limitations bhi hain.

## 1. Security

Sabse important known limitations mein se ek **security** hai.

AI agent ke behind Large Language Model hota hai.

Agar accounting data jaise sensitive information ko AI agent se process karna hai, to data ko model tak bhejna pad sakta hai.

Companies ke liye question hota hai:

**"Mera sensitive data kitna secure hai?"**

Banks aur other organisations especially data security ko lekar cautious hain.

Isi wajah se kuch companies Agentic AI aur Generative AI ko har field mein immediately implement nahi kar rahi hain.

Kuch teams quickly adopt kar rahi hain, jabki kuch teams abhi observe aur evaluate kar rahi hain.

---

## 2. Inconsistent / Unpredictable Behaviour

AI agents ke paas reasoning capability hoti hai.

Kabhi-kabhi ye reasoning loop mein ja sakti hai.

Human situation ko stop kar sakta hai, lekin AI agent kabhi-kabhi inconsistent behaviour dikha sakta hai.

Agar agent ek mistake karta hai, to kabhi-kabhi woh us mistake ko continue kar sakta hai.

Agentic AI abhi comparatively early stage mein hai, isliye inconsistency ek known issue hai.

### Spanish App Example

Suppose aapne 4–5 din mein ek Spanish learning app build ki.

Aapne multiple features add kar diye.

App almost ready hai.

Ab aap sirf ek instruction dete ho:

**"Launching soon" ka ek small notification add karo.**

Kabhi-kabhi AI agent unpredictable behaviour dikha sakta hai.

Woh:

- Existing code change kar sakta hai
- System break kar sakta hai
- Features remove kar sakta hai
- Kabhi-kabhi complete app ko change karke sirf notification rakh sakta hai

Aise cases mein version maintain karna useful hai taaki previous version par wapas ja sakein.

---

# 3. Cost

AI agents currently costly tools ho sakte hain.

Especially enterprise level par cost ek important consideration hai.

Future mein pricing comparatively reasonable ho sakti hai.

Isliye Agentic AI implement karte waqt cost bhi evaluate karna important hai.

# Guardrails

AI agent ke unpredictable behaviour ko control karne ke liye **Guardrails** ka concept use kiya ja raha hai.

Aap agent ko certain boundaries de sakte ho:

- Sirf specified premises mein kaam karo
- Unrelated cheezon ko touch mat karo
- Defined instructions ke bahar mat jao

Ye unpredictable behaviour ko control karne ke methods mein se ek hai.

# Prompt Engineering aur AI Agent Behaviour

Question:

**Kya prompt engineering ke through AI agent ko control kiya ja sakta hai?**

Answer:

Haan, prompt engineering ek important approach hai.

Prompt ka matlab hai AI ko diya gaya input/instruction.

Prompt ko is tarah likhna chahiye ki AI agent requirements ko incorrectly interpret na kare.

---

## Short Prompt Ki Problem

Spanish app ke liye initial prompt bahut simple tha:

> Spanish language learn karne ke liye simple games wali app ka end-to-end code likho.

Ye prompt shallow tha.

Ismein clearly specify nahi tha:

- Kaunsi programming language use karni hai
- Kaunsa framework use karna hai
- Mobile ya web platform kya hai
- Backend kya hona chahiye
- Application architecture kya honi chahiye

Isliye AI agent unpredictable behaviour kar sakta hai.

---

# Detailed Prompt

Better approach hai ki detailed prompt diya jaaye.

Example requirements:

- Senior full-stack engineer ki tarah work karo
- Production-quality end-to-end code/scaffolding do
- Target platform define karo
- Mobile primary hai
- React Native + Expo + TypeScript ya Flutter
- Backend requirements define karo
- Core modules define karo
- Logic define karo
- Data/content define karo
- Analytics define karo
- A/B testing requirements define karo
- Sample code include karo

Jitna clearly requirements define ki jaayengi, utna AI agent ke predictable behaviour ke chances better honge.

Agar specifically React Native framework use karna hai, to agent ko wahi framework follow karne ke liye clearly batana chahiye.

# Reinforcement Learning aur Agentic AI

Question:

**Reinforcement Learning Agentic AI mein kaise fit hoti hai?**

Answer ke according, Machine Learning, Deep Learning aur Reinforcement Learning jaise methods Large Language Models build karne mein use ho sakte hain.

Agentic AI ko **Large Language Model ke upar ek application layer** ke roop mein explain kiya gaya.

Example:

Suppose ek model predict karta hai ki koi social-media comment:

- Offensive hai
- Ya non-offensive

Model banane ke liye ML/DL/Reinforcement Learning jaise approaches use ho sakti hain.

Yaani model prediction provide karega aur agent us output ke basis par further actions perform kar sakta hai.

---

# Reinforcement Learning Ka "Agent" vs Agentic AI Ka "Agent"

Yahaan ek important clarification diya gaya:

**Reinforcement Learning mein bhi "agent" terminology use hoti hai.**

Lekin:

> Reinforcement Learning ka agent aur Agentic AI ka agent same nahi hai.

Sirf naam same hai.

Reinforcement Learning mein agent environment ke saath interact karke learning karta hai aur trial-and-error approach use karta hai.

Agentic AI mein AI agent ek application-level system hai jo goal achieve karne ke liye actions perform karta hai.

Dono mein similarity ho sakti hai, lekin dono same concept nahi hain.

# Complex Problems Mein Overall Approach

Agar kisi task ko manually 50–60 humans ki team perform kar rahi ho, to pehle complete process ko break down karna padega:

1. Overall problem identify karo
2. Process ko multiple steps mein divide karo
3. Har team ka responsibility define karo
4. Human workflow ko document karo
5. Jahan appropriate ho, wahan human task ke replacement mein AI agent design karo
6. Multiple agents ko required workflow ke according organise karo

Software creation mein kaafi process already documented hota hai, isliye AI agents ke through software-related tasks automate karna comparatively straightforward example ho sakta hai.

Healthcare jaise complex domains mein proper planning, domain knowledge aur overall problem-solving approach ki zaroorat hogi.

# Summary

- **Agentic AI** ko aise AI systems ke roop mein explain kiya gaya hai jo ek given goal ko achieve karne ke liye independently multiple tasks perform kar sakte hain.
- AI agents data ko perceive, understand, reason aur decisions ke through action le sakte hain.

- Complex domains jaise healthcare mein ek single agent ke bajay multiple agents aur proper project planning ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.
- Overall, Agentic AI ko **Large Language Model ke upar application-level layer** ke roop mein explain kiya gaya hai.
